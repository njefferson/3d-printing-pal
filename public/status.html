What is built

  • The app. Job kanban, filament inventory, model catalog, all in the browser on your device. Remaining weight is computed on every read and never stored, so there is no field that can drift.
  • Data safety. Export is one timestamped file. Import replaces everything, takes a safety copy first — to a download and to the database, because a download can fail silently — and the clear-and-refill is a single transaction.
  • Undo. One press reverses a whole change including everything it cascaded into: delete a spool four jobs drew on and all five records come back together.
  • Reordering without dragging. The Move panel sets a card's place inside its column as well as which column it is in.
  • A request arrives as a link. Paste the address someone sent and the name fills itself in, the model fills in from the name, and the address is kept with the model. Cards carry a button to the site.
  • Room on the board. A job with no picture no longer leaves a picture-sized gap, and carries a small mark with its initial so it still registers beside a card with a photograph.
  • One screen does the whole job. A picture can be added where the job is made, and the app says which record keeps it. A model can start its own job. Naming a model fills the title. One paste catalogues a model.
  • Undo is a button in the top bar, beside the (i), where every other program on the device keeps it. It is there from the moment the app opens rather than appearing after your first change, it says what it would put back, and nothing has to be dismissed. The band across the page is gone.
  • The job type is a real question with four real answers. Ordered, Asked, Gift and Fun sort by who it is for, whether they asked, and whether they are paying — and each one changes what the form asks and what the card shows, rather than only the colour of a badge.

Found and not fixed

A page that only lists wins is an advertisement.

  • A red gate does not stop a deploy. Gates and Deploy are separate workflows with no dependency, so a build whose gates fail still deploys. The fix couples a deploy to a gate, which is exactly how a release silently stops arriving — so that trade is yours to make.
  • Two devices cannot both write. Import replaces everything, so passing one file back and forth loses whatever the other device added. A phone that only ever CAPTURES and a computer that owns everything is the shape that works, and it needs an add-only import that does not exist yet.
  • A card does not say how long something has been waiting, what it is worth, or whether there is filament for it. Picking what to print next is still a judgement made from a name and a picture.
  • Undo lasts only while the app is open. Closing it or reloading starts again with nothing to undo, and restoring a backup clears it.
  • A picture has to be added by hand. It can be pasted where the job is made now, so it is one screen rather than two — but no address fills it in, and for MakerWorld that is now measured rather than assumed. See below.
  • iPadOS will not always let a waiting update take over while the app is open. Closing it fully and reopening does it.

Checked, and not a defect

Things that look like gaps and are decisions.

  • Dragging a picture out of a browser into the app works. A session said it did not; a drop probe on a real machine showed the browser hands over the file. Printables is the exception — its carousel swallows the drag, so that site wants copy-and-paste.
  • The address is kept on the model, not the job. The model is the thing that exists on somebody else's site; the job is one instance of printing it. A copy per job would be one address written down many times, drifting apart the moment one was corrected.
  • The Model box on a job never renames a model. Editing it points the job at a different model, or makes one. Renaming happens in Models.
  • Pictures are copied in, never hotlinked. Pointing at the original would tell that site every time your board is drawn, break when their CDN moved, and vanish offline.
  • Fetching a picture from a MakerWorld link is not possible, and it is their decision rather than ours. Measured on your machine: their image server shows the picture to anyone — it displayed at 1000×750 — and permits nobody to read its bytes. So there is nothing the app could copy in, and the only thing left would be pointing at their server, which tells them every time your board is drawn, breaks when their CDN moves, and shows nothing offline. Other sites each have their own answer; this one is closed.
  • Three of the four addresses tried were PAGE addresses, and the probe said so rather than reporting them as refusals. That distinction is the difference between closing this question and closing it wrongly — a page address failing looks exactly like a picture being refused.
  • Remaining weight has no field anywhere. It is recomputed from the jobs on every read, which is why nothing can drift it.
