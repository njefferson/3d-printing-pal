      About print-tracker

      What this is

      A place to keep your 3D printing straight: what you are printing and who for, how much filament is left, and which models have earned their keep. It is a board of jobs, a shelf of spools and a catalogue of models, and it runs entirely on this device.

      Your data lives in this browser, and nowhere else

      There is no account and no server. Nothing you type leaves the device, nobody else can see it — including whoever made this — and there is nothing to sign up for.

      That also means it is not backed up. Clearing this site's data, or deleting the browser, takes everything with it. Press the i at the top, then Your data, and export a copy now and then — keep the file somewhere you trust. It is one file, and importing it puts everything back exactly as it was.

      What it is not

      • It is not a slicer and it never talks to your printer. The printer box is a note to yourself about which machine a job is on.
      • It is not tied to any brand. Nothing here breaks when you change machines.
      • It is not a shop or an invoice book. It records what you charged; it does not send anybody a bill.

      Put it on your home screen

      Worth doing first — it opens like any other app afterwards, and works with no signal.

      • iPhone and iPad, in Safari: press Share, then Add to Home Screen.
      • Android, in Chrome: press the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
      • Windows or macOS, in Chrome or Edge: open the three-dot menu, then Cast, save and share or Apps, then Install this page as an app.
      • macOS, in Safari: open the File menu, then Add to Dock.

      Two things that are not guessable

      A spool's remaining weight is never typed in. It is the spool's total minus every gram you have logged against a job, worked out fresh each time you look — so it cannot drift, and there is no number to correct. Log the grams on the job and the shelf follows.

      Every job answers one question: who is it for, and are they paying? Ordered is a print somebody is paying for — it asks who for and what you charged. Asked is the same favour with no money in it. Gift is for somebody who did not ask and may not know. Fun is yours. The buttons above the board light up for the kinds you are looking at, so you can see only what people are waiting on.

      If you get stuck

      This welcome stays under the i button at the top. Pressing it opens a short list: this page, what changed in each release, your data and its safety copies, what to do when something is wrong, and the accessibility statement. Nothing here is a one-time message you have to finish reading now.

      Keep a copy

      There is no account and no server, so nothing here is backed up anywhere. Clearing this site's data, or deleting the browser, takes the lot. An export is one file with everything in it — jobs, spools, models and pictures — and importing it puts everything back exactly as it was.

      Your safety copies

      Before any import replaces your data, the app saves a copy of what is already here — both as a download and inside the app. The most recent are below.

      Where the numbers come from

      All of them come from you. This app fetches nothing, ships with no dataset, and contacts no service — so there is no feed to credit and nothing to go out of date behind your back. Every figure on screen is either something you typed or arithmetic on it.

      How to report a problem

      Open the diagnostic report, copy it, and send it with a note about what happened. It is plain text, it says what the app can see about this device and this build, and it contains nothing you have typed into the app — no job titles, no names, no notes.

      The version number at the bottom of the screen opens the same report.

      If the app looks out of date

      A new version never takes over while you are using it — it waits, and a strip appears at the top of the screen offering it. On an installed app on iPadOS, closing it fully and opening it again is what lets a waiting version through.
